Chapter I — The Opening Scene
"Some people don't just arrive in the world —
they rearrange it."
You were born and the universe quietly shifted its weight. Not with trumpets or grand announcements — just a soft, certain knowing that something luminous had arrived. This is the story of that arrival. Of a girl who became a force. Who laughed so easily it made strangers feel like they'd known her forever. Who carried warmth like it was the most natural thing in the world — because for her, it was.

This is your film, Sakshi. Every frame of it belongs to you.

YOUR FUTURE
Wishes written in starlight
01
That you chase the terrifying thing
The dream that lives in the back of your mind — the one you haven't said out loud yet. I hope this is the year you say it out loud. And then go do it.
02
That people love you the way you love them
You give so freely. You show up when it's inconvenient. You remember the little things. I hope the world reflects that back to you, in full.
03
That you trust yourself more than you already do
Your instincts are good. Your heart is right most of the time. The version of you who acts on that — she's unstoppable.
04
That you get the trip, the meal, the midnight laugh
All the small, perfect moments. The ones you almost didn't do. I hope you do every single one of them this year.
05
That you remember — you are the good thing
You've been the reason other people's days got better for as long as I've known you. You deserve to feel that about yourself. Today. Tomorrow. Always.
